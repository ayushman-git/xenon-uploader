Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

XenOn Uploader is a personal automation tool run by the owner of the XenOn YouTube channel. It is used only with that channel's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's consent (Google OAuth), it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish schedules, and to read the channel's own video and analytics data.

What it stores

An OAuth refresh token and the channel's own video metadata are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
